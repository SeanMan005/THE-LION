Why attachments exceed records

647 index entries sit under 36 shards, but only 428 distinct documents exist. A record is placed in every shard it genuinely load-bears in, so the bars on fig. 1 measure shard load, not a partition of the corpus.

The hottest junctions

A3 (The Single Dial), M2 (Iteration & the Choke Point) and O3 (Power & Positioning) each answer to four separate index views — the highest fan-in in the system, and the shards most expensive to get wrong.

The deepest shard

M10 (Silicon Absorption) holds 50 records, more than any other and more than the whole OUTPUTS partition holds distinct. It is a hot-store shard with one view reaching it, which is the signature of material that has not been compacted yet.
